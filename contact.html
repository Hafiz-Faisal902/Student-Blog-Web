Get in touch

Found a mistake in a post, have a topic you want me to cover, or just want to say hi? Fill this in and it'll open in your email app, addressed to me.

Heads up: open script.js and find the line const CONTACT_EMAIL = ... near the top of the contact form section — swap in your real email address there before you publish. Also, this only works in a real browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) — code-editor preview panes like VS Code's don't support opening email links. And since it opens the visitor's own email app, it only works if they have one set up — if you want a form that submits without that (and lets people leave public comments), a free service like Formspree or a comment widget like giscus (once your site is on GitHub) are good next steps.
