Hi, I'm Hafiz.

I'm a student, and this site is where I write about what I'm learning — starting with networking, since that's what I'm studying right now, but it won't stay just that. Whatever comes up in class or in a side project that I think is worth explaining, I'll write it up here.

The goal isn't to sound like a textbook. It's to explain things the way I'd want them explained to me: plain language, real comparisons, and no pretending something makes sense before it actually does.

I built this site myself — no page builder, no AI-generated content — just HTML, CSS, and a bit of JavaScript, and I'm learning more of all three with every post I add. If you're a classmate or a teacher checking this out, thanks for stopping by. More posts are on the way.
